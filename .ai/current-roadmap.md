# GGCCC CMS Roadmap

This document represents the current development status of GGCCC CMS.

Its purpose is to help developers and AI assistants understand:

- what already exists
- what is currently being built
- what has been approved
- what remains an idea

---

# Project Status

Current Phase

🚧 Active Development

Overall Goal

Build a complete Church Management System centered around discipleship and leadership development.

---

# Status Legend

✅ Implemented

Feature exists in the current application.

🚧 Planned

Feature has been designed and approved but has not yet been completed.

💡 Future Idea

Idea worth remembering but not yet committed.

# Training Management System (TMS)

Status

🚧 Planned

Priority

Highest

Current Active Implementation

- Enrollment-scoped Training Guide assignment and Guide history
- Cell Group and Cell Leader enrollment snapshots
- Configurable per-program, per-session requirement progress for SUYNL and
  LifeClass

Future

- Training Migration after source-data review
- Bulk Add Members remains separate and may later support a reviewed migration
  workflow

Purpose

The operational heart of GGCCC CMS.

The Training Management System manages the complete discipleship journey of every member.

Planned Features

- Enrollment
- Attendance
- Assignments
- Remedials

# Guiding Principle

Whenever choosing the next feature, ask:

Does this improve discipleship?

Does this reduce administrative work?

Does this strengthen the Training Management System?

If the answer is yes, it is likely a good investment.
