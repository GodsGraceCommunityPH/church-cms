# Church Life Gallery Curation

The source ZIP archives remain unchanged outside the repository. The website uses a curated set of 101 photos, each exported as a small grid thumbnail and a larger WebP lightbox image.

## Source review

| Album | Still images reviewed | Displayed | Notes |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Lighthouse | 96 | 20 | Excluded repetitive photo-booth poses, rotated frames, and weak crops. |
| Camp — Day 1 | 128 | 18 | Excluded 37 MOV videos, dark campfire frames, repeated baptism frames, and weak crops. |
| Camp — Day 2 | 23 | 15 | Excluded 40 MOV videos and repeated group-photo frames. |
| 40th Anniversary | 244 | 24 | Excluded repetitive worship frames, obstructed faces, blur, and awkward crops. |
| 41st Anniversary | 213 | 24 | Excluded repetitive activity frames, obstructed faces, blur, and awkward crops. |

The automated review found no byte-for-byte duplicate stills. It flagged five visually similar camp pairs; only the strongest representative frames were retained. `.DS_Store` and all videos were excluded from display. No original was deleted.

## Homepage hero candidates

These candidates are reported for Product Owner review only. The current homepage hero was not changed.

1. **IMG_2489.JPG — GGCCC 41st Anniversary**
   - Landscape, wide full-congregation portrait.
   - Strongest overall group coverage and clear sense of church community.
   - The light upper wall provides usable text space, especially on the left and right edges.
   - Crops reasonably on mobile, though the center congregation should remain the focal point.

2. **IMG_2487.JPG — GGCCC 41st Anniversary**
   - Landscape, wide congregation portrait from a slightly tighter position.
   - Faces remain readable while still communicating the scale of the gathering.
   - Clean upper background can support a dark gradient and light text.
   - Mobile crop works best centered on the middle rows.

3. **IMG_2486.JPG — GGCCC 41st Anniversary**
   - Landscape, very wide congregation portrait.
   - Offers the most horizontal breathing room for desktop hero copy.
   - Text placement is practical over a controlled edge gradient.
   - Mobile cropping is acceptable but needs a centered focal position to avoid cutting outer groups.

The 40th Anniversary archive is rich in worship and fellowship moments, but it does not contain a congregation-wide frame as strong or as hero-friendly as these three 41st Anniversary photographs.
