# Domain Glossary

# Status

🚧 Active

---

# Purpose

This document defines the official business vocabulary of GGCCC CMS.

Every module should use these definitions consistently.

If a term is not defined here, it should not become part of the system until its meaning has been agreed upon.

---

# Core Principle

Words matter.

Every business term should have one meaning throughout the application.

Avoid using multiple words to describe the same concept.

---

# Visitor

A person who has interacted with the church but is not yet recognized as a church member.

Examples:

- Sunday visitor
- Online registration
- Outreach contact

A Visitor is not yet a Member.

---

# Member

A person officially recognized by the church.

A Member may or may not:

- belong to a Cell Group
- serve in a Ministry
- participate in Training

These relationships are independent.

---

# Cell Group

A small discipleship community.

A Cell Group exists to disciple Members.

It is not an administrative department.

---

# Cell Leader

The leader responsible for shepherding a Cell Group.

A Cell Leader monitors spiritual growth rather than administrative performance.

---

# Ministry

A service area within the church.

Examples include:

- Music Team
- Technical Team
- Training Team
- Food Ministry
- Finance
- Administration

Serving in a Ministry is independent of Training completion unless leadership establishes a prerequisite.

---

# Training Program

A reusable curriculum.

Examples:

- SUYNL
- Life Class
- SOL 1
- SOL 2
- SOL 3

A Training Program is **not** a scheduled class.

Think of it as a course definition.

---

# Training Class

A scheduled offering of a Training Program.

Examples:

Life Class — January 2027

Life Class — April 2027

SOL 1 — August 2027

Multiple Training Classes may exist for the same Training Program.

---

# Enrollment

Represents one Member participating in one Training Class.

Enrollment begins when leadership places a Member into a class.

Enrollment is the parent record for attendance, assignments, notes, recommendations, and completion.

---

# Attendance

Participation in an individual class session.

Attendance is recorded per meeting.

Attendance alone never determines graduation.

---

# Assignment

Work completed by the Member as part of a Training Class.

Assignments measure participation and understanding.

Assignments remain separate from attendance.

---

# Remedial

Additional work assigned to satisfy incomplete training requirements.

Remedials supplement the original class.

They do not replace historical attendance.

---

# Trainer

The individual responsible for teaching a Training Class and evaluating enrolled Members.

A Trainer may conduct multiple Training Classes.

---

# Recommendation

A Trainer's assessment of whether a Member appears ready for advancement.

Recommendation is advisory.

It is not approval.

---

# Approval

The final leadership decision regarding advancement.

Approval belongs to church leadership.

Approval may differ from the Trainer's recommendation.

---

# Completion

Confirmation that a Member has successfully completed a Training Class.

Completion becomes permanent historical information.

Completion does not automatically enroll the Member into another class.

---

# Progression

Movement from one Training Program to another.

Progression is always a leadership decision.

The application may suggest progression.

The application must never perform progression automatically.

---

# Historical Record

Information that should be preserved for reporting and auditing.

Examples:

- Completed trainings
- Attendance
- Trainer notes
- Recommendations
- Cell Group transfers

Historical records should rarely be deleted.

---

# Role

A responsibility assigned to a User.

Examples:

- Administrator
- Pastor
- Trainer
- Cell Leader

A User may hold multiple Roles.

Roles determine permissions.

---

# User

An authenticated person who can access the application.

A User account is separate from a Member record.

Examples:

- Administrator
- Pastor
- Trainer

In future versions, not every Member will necessarily have a User account.

---

# Guiding Principle

Whenever a new feature introduces a new business term:

1. Add it to this glossary.
2. Define it clearly.
3. Use that definition consistently throughout the application.
