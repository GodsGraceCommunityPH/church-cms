# Training Management System (TMS)

# Status

🚧 Planned

Priority: Highest

---

# Overview

The Training Management System (TMS) is the operational core of GGCCC CMS.

Its purpose is to help church leaders manage the discipleship journey of every member through structured training, progress tracking, leadership recommendations, and historical records.

Unlike a Learning Management System (LMS), the TMS is not designed for online education.

Instead, it supports classroom-based discipleship within the church.

---

# Objectives

The system should answer questions such as:

- What training has this member completed?
- What class is the member currently taking?
- Who is the trainer?
- Has the member completed required assignments?
- Does the member need remedial work?
- Is the member ready for the next class?
- Why has the member not advanced?

---

# Training Philosophy

Training progression is intentionally based on leadership.

The application assists decision-making.

It does not replace it.

Completion alone does not qualify a member for advancement.

Leadership assessment remains the final authority.

---

# Training Path

Current training sequence:

1. SUYNL

# Key Concepts

The following concepts are independent of each other.

## Enrollment

A member has been accepted into a class.

Enrollment does not imply attendance.

---

## Attendance

Attendance records participation.

Attendance alone does not imply completion.

---

## Assignments

Assignments measure understanding and participation.

Assignments may be submitted after class.

---

## Remedials

Remedials allow members to complete missed or incomplete requirements.

Remedials should preserve the original attendance history.

---

## Completion

Completion indicates that the required curriculum has been finished.

Completion does not imply readiness for the next class.

---

## Recommendation

A trainer or leader may recommend advancement.

Recommendation represents leadership assessment.

Recommendation is advisory.

---

## Advancement

Advancement is an administrative decision.

Possible considerations include:

- attendance
- assignments
- trainer observations
- participation
- consistency
- pastoral approval

---

# Roles

## Administrator

Responsible for:

- managing training programs
- managing schedules
- assigning trainers
- enrollment
- reporting

---

## Trainer

Responsible for:

- attendance
- assignments
- remedials
- notes
- recommendations

---

## Cell Leader

Responsible for:

- monitoring member progress
- encouraging participation

Cell Leaders do not modify official training records unless explicitly permitted.

---

## Member

May view:

- completed trainings
- current enrollment
- attendance summary
- assignment status

Members should not edit official records.

---

# Reports

The system should support reports including:

- Members currently enrolled
- Members ready for recommendation
- Members requiring remedials
- Members with incomplete assignments
- Graduated members
- Training history
- Trainer workload

---

# Historical Records

Training history should never be destroyed.

Corrections should preserve previous records whenever practical.

Historical reporting is more important than destructive edits.

---

# Future Enhancements

Potential additions include:

- Certificates
- Digital signatures
- QR attendance
- Class scheduling
- Trainer dashboard
- Email reminders
- SMS reminders
- Progress analytics

---

# Guiding Principle

The TMS exists to support discipleship.

Whenever a feature is proposed, ask:

"Does this help leaders disciple people more effectively?"

If not, reconsider whether the feature belongs in the Training Management System.
