# Member Training Journey

# Status

🚧 Planned

---

# Purpose

This document describes the complete discipleship journey of a person within GGCCC.

It is the primary business workflow for the Training Management System (TMS).

Every feature in the Training module should support this journey.

---

# Philosophy

People move through discipleship.

Training records simply document that journey.

The application exists to help church leaders shepherd people—not to automate spiritual growth.

Leadership always takes precedence over automation.

---

---

# Stage 1 — Visitor

A visitor has attended church but is not yet recognized as a church member.

Possible sources:

- Sunday Service
- Outreach
- Invitation
- Online Registration

Possible next step:

Become a Member.

---

# Stage 2 — Church Member

The individual is recognized by the church.

A member may or may not:

- belong to a Cell Group
- serve in a Ministry
- participate in Training

These are independent relationships.

---

# Stage 3 — Invitation

Leadership determines that the member is ready for training.

The application may display suggested candidates.

The application never invites members automatically.

Invitation is a leadership decision.

---

# Stage 4 — Enrollment

The member is enrolled into a specific Training Class.

Enrollment records:

- class
- trainer
- enrollment date

Enrollment does not imply attendance.

---

# Stage 5 — Attendance

The member attends scheduled sessions.

Attendance is recorded per meeting.

Attendance contributes to evaluation but does not determine graduation.

---

# Stage 6 — Assignments

Assignments demonstrate participation and understanding.

Assignments may be completed:

- during class
- after class
- during remedial sessions

Assignments remain independent from attendance.

---

# Stage 7 — Trainer Evaluation

The trainer evaluates the member using both objective and subjective observations.

Possible considerations include:

- attendance
- participation
- assignments
- faithfulness
- teachability
- leadership potential

Evaluation is not limited to numeric scores.

---

# Stage 8 — Recommendation

The trainer submits a recommendation.

Possible outcomes include:

- Recommend
- Recommend Later
- Not Yet Ready

Recommendations should include optional remarks.

Recommendations guide leadership but do not determine advancement.

---

# Stage 9 — Leadership Approval

Church leadership reviews the recommendation.

Leadership may:

- approve
- defer
- decline

Leadership may override trainer recommendations when necessary.

The system records the final decision.

---

# Stage 10 — Completion

Training is marked complete.

Completion becomes permanent historical information.

Historical records should never be deleted.

---

# Stage 11 — Next Training

Completion may make the member eligible for another training.

Examples

```
SUYNL

↓

Life Class

↓

SOL 1

↓

SOL 2

↓

SOL 3
```

The application should display eligibility.

The application must not automatically enroll members.

---

# Leadership Development

Training is not the final goal.

The desired outcome is leadership development.

Possible future roles include:

- Cell Group Member
- Assistant Cell Leader
- Cell Leader
- Trainer
- Ministry Worker
- Ministry Leader

Training supports leadership growth but does not guarantee leadership appointment.

---

# Exceptional Scenarios

## Withdrawn

Member leaves before completion.

History remains.

Status becomes Withdrawn.

---

## Repeat Training

Leadership may require a member to repeat a Training Program.

Each enrollment remains a separate historical record.

Previous attempts should never be overwritten.

---

## Transfer

Changing Cell Groups does not affect Training History.

Training records belong to the member.

---

## Inactive Member

Training history remains permanently attached to the member even if they become inactive.

---

# Success Metrics

The Training Management System should enable leaders to answer questions such as:

- Where is this member in the discipleship journey?
- What is preventing advancement?
- What training should happen next?
- Who is ready for leadership development?
- Who needs encouragement?
- Who requires follow-up?

---

# Guiding Principle

The objective of GGCCC CMS is not to produce completed classes.

The objective is to help develop mature disciples and future leaders.

Training records exist to support that mission.
